Accessibility Approach

My remediation work focuses on creating documents that are more usable for people who interact with content through different technologies and access methods.

  • Semantic document structure and heading hierarchy
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and graphics
  • Accessible links and descriptive link text
  • Properly structured lists and tables
  • Reading order and logical content sequence
  • Color contrast and visual accessibility
  • Document metadata and language settings
  • Keyboard and assistive technology considerations
  • Accessibility testing and validation
